# Privacy Policy for Dunk-A-Paws

**Last Updated**: September 2026

Thank you for playing **Dunk-A-Paws: Arcade Basketball** ("the Game"), developed by **Tylve Timmermans**. 

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user information.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Use
**Dunk-A-Paws does NOT collect, store, share, or transmit any personal data or personally identifiable information (PII).**

- **No Account Required**: The Game does not require registration, login, or any personal account credentials.
- **No Analytics / Tracking**: We do not use third-party tracking frameworks, advertising identifiers (IDFA), or analytics SDKs that track user activity across apps or websites.
- **No Location Data**: The Game does not access, collect, or store your GPS or geographical location.

---

### 2. Device Permissions
The Game operates entirely offline and does not request access to sensitive device sensors, including your camera, microphone, photo library, or contacts.

---

### 3. Children's Privacy
Because Dunk-A-Paws is a family-friendly game suitable for all ages:
- We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or applicable age in your jurisdiction).
- We fully comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

---

### 4. Local Game Saves
Game progression (such as unlocked levels and high scores) is stored strictly locally on your device using standard iOS local storage (UserDefaults). This data remains on your device and is never uploaded to any remote server.

---

### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be published directly to this page with a revised date.
