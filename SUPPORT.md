## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How do I aim and shoot?
1. Place your finger on your character on the court.
2. Drag backwards to pull the slingshot elastic.
3. Use the dotted trajectory arc to align your angle with the basketball hoop.
4. Release your finger to launch!

### Can I play offline?
**Yes.** Dunk-A-Paws is 100% playable offline without requiring Wi-Fi or cellular data.

### How do I unlock or switch characters?
Tap the **Paw / Character** icon from the main menu or pause screen to switch between available athletes.

### How do I replay levels?
You can access the **Level Select** screen from the home menu to jump back to any level and beat your score.

### Does the game collect any of my personal data?
**No.** Dunk-A-Paws does not collect, store, or track any personal information or analytics. See our [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md) for details.
